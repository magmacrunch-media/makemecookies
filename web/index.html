HOW TO PLAY

one song, one shift

Five machines, none of them waiting for you. Tap a station, or press its number.

1HOPPER
Dump a sack of flour. Overfill it and it goes on the floor.
2MIXER
Press once to start, again to eject. Leave it and the dough toughens.
3BELT
Unstick a jam and boost the line.
4OVEN
Pull the tray golden, not after. Mash to put out a fire.
5PACKING
Ship the box. Four cookies pays double.

RUSH lands four times, on the music. The belt speeds up and every box scores double.

MESS builds from spills, burnt trays and fires. Fill the meter and the inspector freezes the line while the song plays on.

NO LOSING. The shift ends with the song, and is scored on what you shipped:

THE SHIFT

        

          The song is the clock. When it ends, so does your shift — there
          is no losing, only how much you shipped and how much mess you left.
          Fill the MESS bar and the health inspector stops the
          line for four seconds while the music keeps playing.
        

      

      

        
HOW TO PLAY

        

          Keys 1–5 act on the station
          above them; clicking a station works too. Each machine keeps its own
          clock and none of them wait for you. The meter under each bay is how
          close it is to spoiling.
        

      

      

        
SCORING

        

          Golden cookie 100 · overmixed
          45 · raw or burnt 0. A box of
          four pays ×2; shipping inside a RUSH doubles it
          again. Finish under 20 mess for SPOTLESS +500.
